      About

      Furniture for everyday homes

      Nest & Living brings together furniture and bedroom essentials with practical design and sensible prices. Explore the range, compare sizes and finishes, and find a retailer to help with your purchase.

      The collection spans bedrooms, living rooms, dining areas, storage, home details, and outdoor products. Browse by room or product type, then open a product to see its available sizes, colours, materials and details.

      What you can do here

      Start with a room or product type, compare the designs, and choose an exact size and finish. Each product has its own page with images and details you can return to or share.

      • Browse the range by room, category, or product family.
      • Compare the colours, sizes and features of related products.
      • Explore materials and product details to narrow your choices.
      • Use site search when you already know the family, material, or feature you need.

      Easy to explore and share

      Product pages have direct links, so you can save a choice or send it to someone else. Search engines can read the product details too. Even a crawler should be able to establish how wide a bed is.

      You can browse the catalogue without creating an account. Prices, availability, orders and delivery are handled by the retailer; this site helps you decide what to ask about.

      The unusually specific part

      Yes, even 200 centimetres has a page

      Materials, colors, dimensions, package counts, mechanisms, and other published attributes have their own registry pages. That may sound excessive until you need to find every product with the same measurement. Then it suddenly looks less like excess and more like basic civilisation.

      These pages connect the exact value to the product types and families that use it. They are written for comparison, search, and the quiet satisfaction of discovering that “1 package” is indeed an official fact.

      What the catalog can tell you

      • Which variants belong to the same product family.
      • What dimensions, materials, colors, and functions are published.
      • Where a family sits in the wider collection.
      • Which details are genuinely known, rather than decoratively implied.

      What it cannot decide for you

      The catalog can help narrow the field, but it cannot sit on the sofa, inspect your afternoon light, or negotiate with the stairwell. Measurements are useful; your doorway retains veto power.

      Colors also vary between screens and rooms. Use the images for orientation and confirm important finish, delivery, and availability details with the retailer before ordering.

      Implementation notes

      The Cartesian product is our enemy

      Give one sofa six fabrics, four widths, two orientations, three leg finishes, and a few functions, and the tidy product family becomes a combinatorial incident. The Cartesian product is not malicious. It simply has no sense of proportion.

      This site is evidence that the problem can still be defeated with a stubbornly static public solution, as long as enough dynamic machinery works behind it. An internal Webtool resolves families, generates pages, synchronises navigation, prepares search data, and leaves the browser with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that already know their job. Who needs frameworks? Not the delivered page, apparently.

      Need help or more context?

      For customer support and practical questions, continue to the Help Center and FAQ. For product discovery, start from products or collections. Retailers can confirm prices, availability and delivery details.
