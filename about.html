      About

      What Nest & Living is

      Nest & Living is a dedicated product catalog for the Nest & Living collection. The purpose of this site is practical: give the range its own clear structure so products, variants, materials, colors, and specifications are easier to browse than inside a broader store view.

      The collection spans bedrooms, living rooms, dining areas, storage, home details, and outdoor products. Instead of presenting everything as a loose stream of campaigns or listings, this site organizes the range into stable families and category pages that are easier to compare and revisit.

      What you can do here

      The site is built for structured product discovery. You can move from room collections to product groups, from product groups to family pages, and from family pages to individual variants with their own images, dimensions, and structured product details.

      • Browse the range by room, category, or product family.
      • Compare variants, colors, sizes, and attributes on the same family path.
      • Use registry pages for materials, colors, attributes, and specifications.
      • Use site search when you already know the family, material, or feature you need.

      How the collection is described

      Masku describes Nest & Living as a collection built around accessible design, everyday usability, and a calm Scandinavian look. That direction is useful here too, but the site itself stays grounded: the goal is not to oversell, but to make the range understandable.

      In practice that means clearer family naming, practical product data, stable image paths, and direct links between what a product is, how it looks, and where it fits in the broader collection.

      Why this site exists

      Nest & Living is maintained as a static site on purpose. That keeps the public experience fast, simple, and crawlable while still supporting richer filtering, structured data, product registries, and family-level navigation.

      Behind the scenes, the site is maintained with an internal Webtool that keeps navigation, images, registries, search data, product-family artifacts, and public page structure in sync. The public side stays lightweight even when the catalog itself grows.

      Need help or more context?

      For customer support and practical questions, continue to the help and FAQ pages. If you want to browse the full range directly, start from products or collections.
