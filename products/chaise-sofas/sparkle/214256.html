      Sparkle Chaise Sofa, Beige, Left

      Sparkle Chaise Sofa in Beige with a left-facing layout, sized 242 × 153 × 71 cm and specified with Comfort foam. Made from Fabric, Foam, Metal, and Plywood.

      SKU 214256 242 × 153 cm / Beige / 5 other variants

      The Sparkle family brings a chaise sofa format in a roomy 242 × 153 × 71 cm size, designed in left- and right-facing options to suit different room layouts. Each piece is finished in one of three colours: Beige, Brown, or Green. All models are specified with Comfort foam and use Fabric, Foam, Metal, and Plywood. The collection gives a straightforward choice of handedness and colour while keeping the overall look and sizing consistent across the range.
