      Meta Sideboard

      Meta sideboards bring a simple, cabinet-style storage profile in 140 × 45 × 76 cm, offered in natural oak or brown with MDF and wood veneer.

      2 variants Featured SKU 209405

      The Meta sideboard family offers a clean-lined storage piece for dining rooms, living rooms, or hallways. Each design measures 140 × 45 × 76 cm and uses MDF with wood veneer, creating a calm, straightforward look that suits everyday storage needs. The collection is available in a natural oak finish and a brown version, allowing you to choose the tone that best fits your interior.
