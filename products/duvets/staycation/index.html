      Staycation Duvet

      White Staycation duvets in Cotton, Polyester, offered in two sizes for everyday bedding needs.

      2 variants Featured SKU 220236

      The Staycation duvet family offers two white options for the bed, both made from Cotton, Polyester. Choose the size that suits your bedding setup: 200 × 150 × 5 cm for a smaller cover or 200 × 220 × 5 cm for a larger one. With a simple white finish, these duvets fit easily into a range of bedroom styles and pair well with different bedding looks. The family keeps the same material and colour across both sizes, while giving you a choice of dimensions for your preferred bed size.
