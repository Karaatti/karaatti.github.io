      Wooly Duvet

      Wooly duvets in White combine Cotton and Wool in a simple, versatile layer for the bed.

      Single variant SKU 221937 150 × 200 cm / White

      The Wooly family offers a duvet in a clean White finish with a soft, understated look for everyday sleeping spaces. Its Cotton and Wool material combination gives the range a natural, familiar character that suits a calm bedroom setting. Sized at 150 × 200 × 1 cm, it is a practical choice for use on a single bed or as a lighter covering layer. The simple appearance makes it easy to pair with different bedding styles, whether kept minimal or layered with other textiles.
