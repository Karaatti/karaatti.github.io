      Bacoli Dining Table

      Bacoli Dining Table in a natural oak look, sized 200 × 95 × 75 cm, with a spacious rectangular top for dining and entertaining.

      SKU 209395 95 × 200 cm / Beige

      The Bacoli dining table is designed for shared meals, relaxed hosting and everyday use in the dining room. Its 200 × 95 × 75 cm size offers a spacious rectangular surface, while the natural oak appearance brings a warm, easy-to-style look to the room. Made with Particle board, MDF, Melamine, Plywood, Wood veneer, it combines several materials in a clean, contemporary table form. The Bacoli family suits interiors that call for a practical dining centrepiece with a natural finish.
