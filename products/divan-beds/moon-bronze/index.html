      Moon Bronze Divan Bed

      Divan bed with 5-zone pocket spring support and a durable wooden frame. Comes with smoked ash tapered legs and a choice of sizes.

      4 variants Featured SKU 197653

      The Moon Bronze divan bed offers balanced support with its 5-zone pocket spring system. Its frame is made from durable wood and it stands on smoked ash tapered legs that add lightness and ease of cleaning. Available in several sizes, it can be complemented with optional toppers and headboards to suit individual needs.
