      Freedom Three-seater Sofa

      Freedom three-seater sofas offer a clean, contemporary look for living spaces, with a spacious profile and a white finish that suits many interiors.

      Single variant SKU 198420 216 × 97 cm / White

      The Freedom family is a range of three-seater sofas designed for living room seating. Its profile combines a generous format with a simple, contemporary expression, making it suitable for relaxed everyday use and larger lounge settings. This sofa is presented in white and measures 216 × 97 × 72 cm. It is made from fabric, metal, plywood, and polyester, giving it a material mix that supports its tailored, modern appearance.
