      Moon Bronze continental bed, 120 x 200 cm

      Grey, 120 x 200 cm. Pocket spring support with durable wooden frame and black wooden legs.

      SKU 197737 120 x 200 cm / Grey / 3 other variants

      Moon Bronze Continental Bed provides a balanced combination of comfort and timeless design. It has a pocket spring system without zones and a sturdy wooden frame with a 4000-day quality guarantee. The bed base comes in parts but is easy to assemble. Black wooden legs add a light touch and make cleaning simple. The bed can be customized with various mattress toppers and headboards to suit your needs.
