      Moon Silver Continental Bed, Beige, 200 × 200 cm

      Beige, 200 x 200 cm. 5-zone pocket spring continental bed with high wooden legs.

      SKU 200091 200 × 200 cm / Beige / 9 other variants

      The Moon Silver continental bed combines comfort and timeless style for the bedroom. It features a 5-zone pocket spring system that supports the body individually. The bed frame is made from durable wood and chipboard, complemented by high wooden legs that make cleaning simple. Optional headboards and mattress toppers are available to tailor the bed to your needs.
