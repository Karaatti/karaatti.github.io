      Meta Coffee Table, Brown, 60 × 120 cm

      Meta Coffee Table in Brown, sized 120 × 60 × 45 cm, with MDF, Wood veneer. A rectangular format with a clear, streamlined look.

      SKU 209410 60 × 120 cm / Brown / 3 other variants

      The Meta coffee table family brings together pared-back shapes in a practical range of sizes for different living room layouts. Round and rectangular options make it easy to choose a table that suits the scale and arrangement of your seating area. Each design is offered in MDF, Wood veneer, with selected versions in Brown. The collection includes compact and longer formats, giving the Meta range a consistent look across multiple table proportions.
