      Meta TV Stand

      Meta TV stands offer a wide 180 cm profile for media setups, with a lowboard format and a clean wood-veneer look.

      2 variants Featured SKU 209403

      The Meta TV Stand family is designed as a lowboard for living room media storage and display. Both pieces measure 180 × 40 × 55.6 cm and are made from MDF, Wood veneer, giving the range a pared-back, natural material character. One version highlights a natural oak look, while the other brings a darker brown tone inspired by dark espresso oak. The shared proportions suit larger televisions and help create a balanced, horizontal presence in the room.
