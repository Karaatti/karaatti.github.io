      Bacoli Cabinet

      Bacoli Storage Cabinet in a natural oak look, with four doors and a tall enclosed format. Measures 90 × 40 × 140 cm and is made from Particle board, MDF, Melamine, Plywood, Wood veneer.

      SKU 209396 90 × 40 cm / Beige

      The Bacoli storage cabinet line is designed for organised storage in a clean, natural oak style. Its enclosed form suits living rooms, dining areas, hallways, or other spaces where you want items kept out of sight while remaining close at hand. The family includes a four-door cabinet format that gives a substantial storage presence without adding unnecessary visual clutter. The cabinet measures 90 × 40 × 140 cm and is made from Particle board, MDF, Melamine, Plywood, Wood veneer. It combines a straightforward silhouette with a natural oak finish for an understated look that works easily with many interiors.
