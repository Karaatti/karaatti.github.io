      Glint Rug

      Glint rugs bring a multicolour look in Chenillepolyester, available in two generous sizes for different room layouts.

      2 variants Featured SKU 220981

      The Glint rug family offers a multicolour finish in Chenillepolyester, bringing an easy way to define a room with colour and pattern. Its low 0.1 cm profile keeps the design visually light while fitting neatly into everyday living spaces. Choose 160 × 230 × 0.1 cm for smaller seating areas or 200 × 290 × 0.1 cm for larger spaces. Both sizes share the same multicolour look and material, making it simple to coordinate across different rooms.
