NEST & LIVING - Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you’ll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and link. Our retailer partners arrange the delivery or pick-up, so feel free to contact them.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery on most unused items in their original condition. Please start your return through the retailer you purchased from. If you have any issues, contact our support team.

How do I submit a warranty claim?

For warranty claims, please reach out first to the retailer where you purchased your product. If they can’t resolve your issue, email us at support@nestliving.dk with your order number and a description of the defect.

Do you ship internationally?

No yet. We only have one partner, Maskun Kalustetalo Oy, who only ships to Finland or Estonia.

Can I change or cancel my order?

If your order hasn’t shipped yet, we may be able to modify or cancel it. Please contact the retailer’s customer service immediately. If you run into any trouble, let us know and we’ll do our best to assist.

How do I care for my NEST & LIVING products?

Each product comes with care instructions on its packaging or user manual. Generally, wipe surfaces with a soft, damp cloth and avoid harsh chemicals. For specific guidance, refer to the manual or our support site.

I’m a designer or entrepreneur. Can I have my product added to the NEST & LIVING collection?

Absolutely! If you’re a creator looking to have your product considered for inclusion in our NEST & LIVING range, please get in touch. Send us your product details, samples (if applicable), and any relevant portfolio or background information. We’ll review your submission and discuss whether it’s a good fit for our collection.

I’m a retailer. How can I carry the NEST & LIVING collection in my store?

We’d love to partner with you! If you’re interested in retailing NEST & LIVING products, please contact our wholesale team. Provide your business details, location, and estimated order volume, and we’ll share our catalog, pricing, and terms of collaboration.
