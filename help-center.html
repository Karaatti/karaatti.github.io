NEST & LIVING Help Center

How to Submit a Complaint

For the fastest resolution to any complaint or warranty claim, please reach out directly to the retailer from whom you purchased your NEST & LIVING product. They have the full details of your transaction and can help process returns, exchanges, or repairs quickly.

Link to Retailer Partners’ Customer Service

For complaints and warranty claims, we recommend starting directly with the retailer where you purchased your NEST & LIVING product. If your retailer is Masku, you can submit your claim here: Masku Returns & Complaints

Still Need Help?

If you encounter any difficulties or your retailer is unable to assist, we’re here for you. Contact our customer support team directly:

When you contact us, please have your order number, retailer name, and a brief description of the issue ready so we can assist you as efficiently as possible.
